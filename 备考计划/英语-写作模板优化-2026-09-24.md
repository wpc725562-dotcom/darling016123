## §2 🔧 通用骨架（所有书信共用 · 9 个字段）

### 字段说明表

| 字段 | 名称 | 必填 | 作用 | 填写要求 |
|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|:--|
| ① | **称呼** | ✅ | 格式分 | 有具体人名就写人名；没有写 `Dear Sir or Madam,`（**注意逗号**） |
| ② | **寒暄** | ⭕ | 凑字 + 自然 | 固定一句 `How is everything going?`（投诉信可省） |
| ③ | **来意** | ✅ | **最关键的 1 句** | `I am writing to ___ .` ← **换这里就换了体裁** |
| ④ | **过渡** | ✅ | 承上启下 | 固定一句 `Here are some relevant details about this matter.` |
| ⑤ | **要点 1** | ✅ | **要点分** | `First and foremost, ___ .` ← 抄题目给的要点① |
| ⑥ | **要点 2** | ✅ | **要点分** | `Furthermore, ___ .` ← 抄要点② |
| ⑦ | **要点 3** | ⭕ | 凑字 | `Last but not least, ___ .` ← 抄要点③（题目只给 2 个就省） |
| ⑧ | **期许** | ✅ | 收尾 | `I sincerely hope ___ .` |
| ⑨ | **客套 + 落款** | ✅ | 格式分 | `I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.` + `Yours sincerely,` + `Li Hua` |

> ⚠️ **③ 来意 和 ⑤⑥⑦ 要点 是得分的命门** —— 其余都是固定句，默写就行。

### 骨架（直接默这段）

```
Dear ______ ,                                              ← ①

How is everything going? I am writing to ______ .          ← ② + ③

Here are some relevant details about this matter.          ← ④
First and foremost, ______ .                               ← ⑤
Furthermore, ______ .                                      ← ⑥
Last but not least, ______ .                               ← ⑦

Taking all these factors into consideration, I sincerely
hope ______ .                                              ← ⑧
I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,
Li Hua                                                     ← ⑨
```

## §3 🔀 只换这一句：5 种「来意」

| 体裁 | ③ 来意句 | ⑤⑥⑦ 要点提示 | 真题 |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| **邀请信 / 邮件** | `I am writing to invite you to ___ .` | 活动时间 / 活动内容 / 期待参与 | 2018 · 2022 · 2024 |
| **求职 / 自荐信** | `I am writing to apply for the position of ___ .` | 教育背景 / 获奖与特长 / 性格与态度 | 2016 · 2023 |
| **投诉信** | `I am writing to complain about ___ .` | 问题一 / 问题二 / 期望的解决办法 | 2017 |
| **劝说信** | `I am writing to express my concern about ___ .` | 危害一 / 危害二 / 我的建议 | 2014 |
| **建议 / 回复邮件** 🆕 | `Thanks for your email asking about ___ . I am writing to give you some suggestions.` | 推荐一 / 推荐二 / 交通方式 | **2026** |

### 3.5 🆕 建议 / 回复邮件（2026 真题 · 要「先回应对方的信」）

**与其余四类的关键差别**：**别人先来信** → 开头必须先**回应对方的信**，不能直接「我写信是为了…」。

**2026 真题原文**：
> 假定你是李华，你的英国好友 Mike 在北京留学，他计划下周来你的城市游玩两天，因此**发来邮件询问你的建议**。请回复，内容包括：
> （1）欢迎 Mike 的到来；（2）推荐游览景区；（3）推荐去往景点的交通方式。**100 个英文单词，不可出现姓名、学校信息。**

**骨架（只改首尾两处）**：

```
Dear Mike,                                          ← ① 称呼（对方先来信 → 用名字）

Thanks for your email asking about ___ .            ← ②🆕 回应对方的信（不是 How is everything going?）
I am writing to give you some suggestions.          ← ③ 来意

Here are some relevant details about this matter.   ← ④ 过渡
First and foremost, ___ .                           ← ⑤ 要点1（欢迎 + 推荐景区）
Furthermore, ___ .                                  ← ⑥ 要点2（交通方式）
Last but not least, ___ .                           ← ⑦ 要点3（祝福 / 期待）

Taking all these factors into consideration, I
sincerely hope ___ .                                ← ⑧ 期许
I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.

Yours sincerely,  Li Hua                            ← ⑨ 落款
```

> ⚠️ **两个坑**（2026 真题明确要求）：
> ① **不可出现姓名、学校信息** —— 别在正文里写自己的真名或学校名（落款写 `Li Hua` 即可）。
> ② **是「回复」，不是「主动写信」** —— 第一句必须是回应对方的信，**不能省**。

**真题范文对照**（可背诵，都是 80–100 词）：

| 年份 | 范文开头 | 范文要点句式 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **2022 邀请信** | `I am writing this letter to express my earnest hope to invite you to visit the exhibition of Traditional Chinese Culture…` | `First and foremost, the exhibition will be held next Friday.` / `Furthermore, you can experience the art of paper-cutting yourself.` / `Last but not least, it will be a meaningful and unforgettable experience.` |
| **2017 投诉信** | `I am writing to complain about the trip organized by your travel agency last week.` | `First, the hotel was dirty and noisy.` / `Second, the food was terrible and the bus was always late.` / `What is more, the guide was impatient.` |
| **2016 求职信** | `I am writing to apply for the position of English teacher in your school.` | `I graduated from … with a bachelor's degree in English.` / `I have won several awards in speech contests and I am good at …` / `I am patient, responsible and love teaching.` |
| **2014 劝说信** | `I am worried about you because you indulge in online games and have failed several courses.` | `Playing games too much not only wastes time but also harms your health and future.` / `I hope you can give up the games, treasure your school years and work hard on your major.` |

> 💡 **注意真题范文有多朴素** —— 没有从句套从句，没有高级词。
> **得分点只有四条：格式正确 · 要点齐全 · 约 100 词 · 少语法硬伤。**
> ⇒ **别追求华丽，追求「不错」。**

## §4 📢 NOTICE 骨架（独立一套 · 4/12 年考过）

| 字段 | 名称 | 必填 | 填写要求 |
|:--:|:--|:--:|:--|
| ① | **标题** | ✅ | `NOTICE` **居中、全大写**（格式分） |
| ② | **吸引注意** | ⭕ | 固定 `May I have your attention, please?` |
| ③ | **事由** | ✅ | `To enrich our campus life, our school is going to organize ___ .` ← 换活动名 |
| ④ | **时间地点** | ✅ | `The event is scheduled to be held in ___ at ___ .` |
| ⑤ | **要点 1** | ✅ | `For one thing, ___ .` |
| ⑥ | **要点 2** | ✅ | `For another, ___ .` |
| ⑦ | **号召** | ✅ | `Those who are interested in it are highly welcome to participate.` |
| ⑧ | **联系方式** | ⭕ | `If you have any questions, please feel free to contact us at ___ .` |
| ⑨ | **发布方 + 日期** | ✅ | **右下角**：`The Student Union` / `Nov. 10th` |

### 4.1 🆕 「改期 / 变更」变体（2025 真题 · 要「致歉」）

**2025 真题原文**：
> 假定你是学生会成员。学生会原定下周五为留学生举办茶话会（tea party），**现因故需要改期**。请你用英文写一则通知（NOTICE），内容包括：
> 1. **对改期表示歉意** 2. 说明改期的原因 3. 说明改期后的时间与地点

**⇒ 与普通通知的差别只有两处**：**开头加致歉**、**时间地点要写「新的」**。

| 位置 | 普通通知 | **改期通知（2025）** |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 事由句后 | （无） | 🆕 `We are sorry to inform you that the event has to be put off.` |
| 时间地点 | `The event is scheduled to be held in ___ at ___ .` | `It will now be held in ___ at ___ instead.` |

**改期版骨架（只加 1 句 + 改 1 句）**：

```
                      NOTICE

May I have your attention, please? To enrich our
campus life, our school is going to organize a tea
party for international students.

We are sorry to inform you that the event has to      ← 🆕 致歉（2025 要求）
be put off.

The reason is that ___ .                              ← 改期原因（2025 要求）

It will now be held in ___ at ___ instead.            ← 新时间地点（注意 instead）

There are many exciting activities waiting for you.
For one thing, ___ . For another, ___ .

Those who are interested in it are highly welcome
to participate.

                                    The Student Union
                                        Nov. 10th
```

> ⚠️ **「因故需要改期」的题目一定要写「原因」** —— 2025 把它列为第 2 条要求。**别只写致歉和改期时间。**

## §5 🧰 万能句库（跨体裁复用 · 按位置分组）

| 位置 | 句子 | 用在 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 寒暄 | `How is everything going?` | 书信（投诉信除外） |
| 过渡 | `Here are some relevant details about this matter.` | 书信 |
| 要点并列 | `First and foremost, …` / `Furthermore, …` / `Last but not least, …` | 书信 |
| 要点并列（简版） | `First, …` / `Second, …` / `What is more, …` | 投诉信（更朴素） |
| 期许 | `Taking all these factors into consideration, I sincerely hope ___ .` | 书信 |
| 客套 | `I would be highly appreciative if you could take my request into consideration.` | 书信 |
| 收尾 | `I am looking forward to hearing from you soon.` | 书信 |
| 收尾（投诉） | `I am looking forward to your early reply.` | 投诉信 |
| 感谢 | `I would be grateful if you could ___ .` | 求职信 |
| 凑字 | `There are many exciting activities waiting for you.` | NOTICE |
| 🆕 **回应来信** | `Thanks for your email asking about ___ .` | 建议 / 回复邮件（2026） |
| 🆕 **致歉** | `We are sorry to inform you that ___ has to be put off.` | NOTICE 改期（2025） |
| 🆕 **替代安排** | `It will now be held in ___ at ___ instead.` | NOTICE 改期（2025） |

> ⚠️ **一篇作文只用 1 个「并列组」** —— 别把 `First and foremost` 和 `First` 混着用。
