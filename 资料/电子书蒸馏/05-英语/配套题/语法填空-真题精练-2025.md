## 原文（已填答案）

Last weekend, as snow fell heavily in North Andover, Firefighter Tim of the North Andover Fire Department responded to **[36] a** call about an accident. Tim **[37] hurried** (hurry) to the scene and discovered a mother and her son inside the vehicle. **[38] Fortunately** (fortunate), the boy and his mother were unharmed. But as the tow truck arrived to take away their damaged car, the child stood in the cold, watching the scene unfold. "He was actually **[39] wearing** (wear) a Marvel coat that night, so I talked to him **[40] about** some superheroes he liked," Tim said. The real superhero of the night was Tim, **[41] who** draped his turnout coat over the little boy as he watched his mother's car get towed away. This simple but **[42] powerful** (power) action that Tim did offered the little boy warmth and comfort in the storm. A photo capturing the moment, **[43] shared** (share) by the fire department on Facebook, quickly gained attention, with commenters praising the firefighter's compassion. "All the **[44] reactions** (reaction) online are incredible. All the positive comments people have left just makes me feel really proud of **[45] myself** (I) and the job I get to do every day," Tim said.

## 逐空解析

| 空号 | 答案 | 有/无提示词 | 考点 | 判定依据（≤30 字） |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| 36 | a | 无 | 冠词 | call可数名词单数首次提及用不定冠词 |
| 37 | hurried | 有 | 时态 | 一般过去时，hurry变y为i加ed |
| 38 | Fortunately | 有 | 词性转换 | 句首作状语，形容词fortunate转副词-ly |
| 39 | wearing | 有 | 时态 | was+V-ing构成过去进行时主动 |
| 40 | about | 无 | 介词 | 固定搭配talk to sb. about sth. |
| 41 | who | 无 | 关系代词 | 非限制性定语从句缺主语先行词指人 |
| 42 | powerful | 有 | 词性转换 | 修饰action作定语，名词power转形容词-ful |
| 43 | shared | 有 | 非谓语 | photo与share被动关系，过去分词作后置定语 |
| 44 | reactions | 有 | 名词单复数 | All the+可数名词，谓语are要求复数 |
| 45 | myself | 有 | 代词 | proud of oneself反身代词主语I对应myself |

## 一句话结论

**词性转换与时态各 2 题最重**；8 有 + 2 无 比例偏向有提示词，**复习重点仍是词形变换 + 动词时态**，无提示词仅考介词搭配与关系代词。
