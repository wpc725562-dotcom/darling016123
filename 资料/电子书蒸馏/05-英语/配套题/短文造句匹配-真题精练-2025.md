## 这个题型到底是什么（先纠正一个常见误解）

考纲写作「短文造句匹配」，**它不是造句，是选句填空**：
一篇短文挖 5 个空，给 A–E 五个句子，一一填回去。

本库 2022–2025 四套真题实测**只有 A–E 五个选项，5 选 5，每个选项恰好用一次**。
（原卷题头写「七选五」，但 PDF 只抽出 5 个选项，F、G 判断为抽取时丢失；
本册按 5 选 5 呈现，真实考试可能是 7 选 5、难度更高，练的时候别依赖「刚好够用」。）

## 原文（已填答案）

Modern life can be stressful, but small changes in your daily routine may help improve your mood. Sometimes a short escape can make you feel better -- this is supported by research showing that physical separation from daily stressors correlates with reduced anxiety levels. **[16] B. Researchers also found that even being away for one night could actually cheer you up.** If you stay away longer, it might even help reduce half of your stress or sadness. The research team, led by psychologist Tim Bush, conducted a study on mental well-being involving over 5,000 participants. **[17] C. After collecting data from the participants, Tim Bush analyzed it carefully.** The results were surprising. **[18] E. One notable finding was that people owning a pet, particularly a cat, could lower the risk of depression by 32% compared to those without pets.** Similarly, non-social interactions like pet ownership showed benefits parallel to social activities in improving moods. The study also examined how people cope with stress. **[19] D. When someone felt overwhelmed, he tended to first withdraw from social interactions.** Then he gradually returned to normal activities after being alone for some time. This behavior highlighted an important psychological principle: your actions directly influence your emotions. **[20] A. What you do shapes what you feel.** If you choose positive activities -- whether social, physical, or even pet-related -- you'll likely feel happier. So, next time you're upset, try going out for a while or spending time with a pet -- it might really help!

## 选项

- A. What you do shapes what you feel.
- B. Researchers also found that even being away for one night could actually cheer you up.
- C. After collecting data from the participants, Tim Bush analyzed it carefully.
- D. When someone felt overwhelmed, he tended to first withdraw from social interactions.
- E. One notable finding was that people owning a pet, particularly a cat, could lower the risk of depression by 32% compared to those without pets.

## 干扰项排除

> **排除理由必须能用「不看答案」的方式判出来。** 凡是写「应放在 X 空」的，都是拿答案反推，
> 做题时用不上。下面每一行都给出可当场验证的依据：主语接不上 / 时序倒置 / 话题偏移 /
> 缺前提 / 正反不合 / 以偏概全。

| 空号 | 排除的选项 | 排除理由（可当场验证的依据） |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 16 | A | A 是全文主旨句。16 空后面紧接 "If you stay away longer" 是**递进**，主旨句会把递进切断 → **位置错（段中不能放总结句）** |
| 16 | C | C 讲「收集完数据后分析」。但 16 空后一句才是 "The research team... conducted a study"，**研究还没开始就说分析完** → **时序倒置** |
| 16 | D | D 的主语是 "he"，16 空前只出现过 research / separation 这类抽象名词，**找不到 he 的先行词** → **代词悬空** |
| 16 | E | E 是「32% 具体数据」，需要前面有一句「结果出人意料」之类的判断来引出。16 空前是方法描述，**没有需要例证的观点** → **无依托** |
| 17 | A | 同 16：主旨句不能放段中，17 空后面还有 "The results were surprising" 要接 → **位置错** |
| 17 | B | B 讲「离开**一晚**」。16 空已经填了同类内容（away for one night），且 16 空后 "stay away **longer**" 是拿它做递进起点，**放 17 就自己递进自己** → **语义循环** |
| 17 | D | 17 空后一句主语是 "The results"，D 带进来的是 "he"，**results 接不上 he** → **主语断裂** |
| 17 | E | E 要靠 "surprising" 引出，而 "The results were surprising" 在 17 空**之后**，放 17 会**先给数据后说意外** → **顺序错** |
| 18 | A | 主旨句，18 空后还有 "Similarly..." 要并列，放总结句会**让并列失去前半句** → **位置错** |
| 18 | B | 18 空后是 "Similarly, non-social interactions **like pet ownership**..."。要用 Similarly，前面必须先出现「宠物」这件事。B 里没有宠物 → **缺前提** |
| 18 | C | 18 空前是 "The results were surprising"，需要的是**具体结果**，C 说的是「分析了数据」这个**动作** → **答非所问** |
| 18 | D | D 带 "he"，18 空后 "Similarly..." 后面没有任何 he 承接，**代词没人接** → **代词悬空** |
| 19 | A | 19 空后还有 "This behavior highlighted an important psychological principle" 才是总结，放主旨句**抢了总结位** → **位置错** |
| 19 | B | 19 空后是 "Then **he** gradually returned"，需要单数人做主语。B 的主语是 "Researchers"（复数）→ **主语不接** |
| 19 | C | C 的主语 Tim Bush 是**研究者**，19 空后 "he" 指的是**被研究的当事人**（先退缩再恢复）。指错人 → **指代错位** |
| 19 | E | E 的主语是 "people owning a pet"（群体），19 空后 "he" 要单数个体 → **主语不接** |
| 20 | B | 20 空后 "If you choose positive activities -- whether social, physical, or even pet-related" 是**总括建议**，B 只说「离开一晚」这一件事，**收不住全文** → **以偏概全** |
| 20 | C | 20 空已在全文末尾，C 回头讲「分析数据」这一研究过程 → **时序倒退** |
| 20 | D | 20 空后是正面建议 "choose **positive** activities"，D 讲的是「退缩」这种负面行为 → **正反不合** |
| 20 | E | 20 空后把三类并列（social / physical / pet-related），E 只讲宠物一类，会把并列**窄化成一类** → **以偏概全** |
