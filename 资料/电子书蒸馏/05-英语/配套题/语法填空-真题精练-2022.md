# 2022 年语法填空真题精练（10 空 / 15 分）

> 来源：历年真题/公共英语/2022.md
> 答案依据：原卷「参考答案 → 语法填空 (36-45)」
> **本卷提示词比例是 8 有 + 2 无**（有：36–41、44、45；无：42、43），
> 与 2023/2024 的 7 有 + 3 无不同。别把「7+3」当成铁律，它是近年常态，不是每年固定。

## 原文（已填答案）

When I was in college, a friend of **[36] mine** (I) told me about a job she had as a server at a restaurant. She said that she had worked there for about a month, but she **[37] finally** (final) decided to quit.

One day, she was serving a table of six people. Everyone seemed friendly and **[38] enjoying** (enjoy) their meal. Then one of the customers asked her to bring more napkins. She quickly went to the back and returned with the napkins. But as she **[39] was handing** (hand) them to the customer, he suddenly grabbed her wrist and pulled her into his lap. She was shocked and pulled away immediately. She was so upset that she **[40] told** (tell) the manager what had happened. The manager said that he would deal with the customer. But when she came back to the table, the customer **[41] was laughing** (laugh) with his friends. He acted as if nothing had happened. She was so angry that she walked away **[42] without** saying a word.

After that shift, she told the manager that she would not work there anymore. The manager said, "Take a few days off and think about **[43] it**." But she had already decided. She was **[44] determined** (determine) to quit. She said, "I am not going to be treated like that. I deserve to be treated with **[45] respect** (respectful)."

## 逐空解析

| 空号 | 答案 | 有无提示词 | 考什么 | 判定依据（≤30 字） |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| 36 | mine | 有（I） | 名词性物主代词 | 介词 of 后接名词性物主代词，固定搭配 |
| 37 | finally | 有（final） | 副词 | 修饰 decided 的副词，表“最终” |
| 38 | enjoying | 有（enjoy） | 现在分词 | 与 friendly 并列表语，表示主动正在进行 |
| 39 | was handing | 有（hand） | 过去进行时 | as 从句谓语，表示当时正在进行的动作 |
| 40 | told | 有（tell） | 一般过去时 | 叙述过去事实，主句谓语用动词过去式 |
| 41 | was laughing | 有（laugh） | 过去进行时 | 表示当时正在进行的动作，与 friends 一起笑 |
| 42 | without | 无 | 介词 | without saying a word 固定搭配 |
| 43 | it | 无 | 代词 | think about 后接宾语，it 指代前文 |
| 44 | determined | 有（determine） | 形容词 | be determined to do 固定搭配，表决心 |
| 45 | respect | 有（respectful） | 名词 | treated with respect 固定搭配，with 后接名词 |
