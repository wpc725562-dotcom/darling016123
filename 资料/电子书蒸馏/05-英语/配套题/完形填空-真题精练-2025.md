> 答案依据：同文件「真题精析」答案区，逐题抽出

> 错因分类：A 词义辨析 / B 固定搭配 / C 语法形式 / D 语篇衔接

## 原文（已填答案）

In a small town, there was a small cat that was very **[21] curious** about the outside world. It often played and explored in the backyard. In November, the **[22] snow** fell heavily, blanketing the town in white. It was very cold outside, but this small cat still visited the backyard. And it **[23] dug** in the snow to find a warmer place. As usual, this small cat went out to play but didn't return for dinner. Its owner was **[24] worried**, searching everywhere but finding no sign of it. Soon the long winter passed. And on one morning of April, the small cat came back, looking happy and **[25] healthy**. It seemed that the small cat had never been **[26] lost** in the past months. The same thing happened again. The small cat disappeared when the cold November arrived, and **[27] reappeared** next spring. The owner was puzzled, and wanted to figure out the **[28] mystery**. Therefore, he decided to **[29] follow** the cat when it went out in another November. Finally, the owner found that the small cat's destination was a nice house. There lived a couple who greeted them warmly. The couple were glad to **[30] explain** all this to the owner. They would travel to the south every winter, and the small cat began to travel with them two **[31] years** ago. The small cat would **[32] run away** when the trip was over, **[33] leaving** their house and returning here some day. Hearing this, the owner realized that this small cat was **[34] smart**. That's because it was a good **[35] way** to escape the cold weather.

## 答案与考点

| 题号 | 答案 | 考什么（≤15字） | 错因 | 一句话说明 |
|:---:|:---:|:---|:---:|---|
| 21 | B | curious about 搭配 | B | eager 接 to do，不接 about，选 curious 符合搭配 |
| 22 | D | 语义复现 snow | D | 上文 white、cold、November 复现雪的语义场 |
| 23 | A | dug 过去式/词义 | A | 叙事过去时需 dug；covered 需被动；slept 矛盾；put 缺宾语 |
| 24 | A | worried 词义/语境 | A | 找不到猫 → 担心，符合语境逻辑 |
| 25 | B | healthy 词义/语境 | A | 猫回来看起来 happy and healthy，排除 lazy/lonely/wealthy |
| 26 | A | lost 词义/语境 | A | never been lost 从未走失，与 disappeared 形成对比 |
| 27 | B | reappeared 时间线 | D | 11 月消失 → 次年春回来，reappeared 唯一符合时间逻辑 |
| 28 | C | mystery 词义/语境 | A | owner puzzled → figure out the mystery，语义衔接 |
| 29 | D | follow 搭配/语境 | B | 决定 follow the cat，固定搭配 follow sb. |
| 30 | B | explain 搭配/语境 | B | couple glad to explain all this，符合告知语境 |
| 31 | D | years 逻辑推理 | D | every winter 与 two years ago 匹配；months 撑不到下一个冬天 |
| 32 | C | run away 词义/语境 | A | 旅行结束后猫 run away 离开，符合情节 |
| 33 | D | leaving 分词并列 | C | leaving 与 returning 并列作分词，唯一语法形式题 |
| 34 | C | smart 评价性词义 | A | 综合前文「找过冬办法」推出聪明，非状态描述 |
| 35 | A | way 词义/语境 | A | a good way to escape，四个名词里只有 way 能表示「办法」 |

## 错因分布

A 词义 ×8 · B 固定搭配 ×3 · C 语法 ×1 · D 语篇衔接 ×3
