============================================================

一、一句话结论
------------------------------------------------------------
十年十次，写作全是**应用文**，一次议论文都没考过。体裁只落在三类：电子邮件、通知、信函；
题目固定 **3 条中文要点**，词数 **80–100**；结构固定"称呼 → 目的句 → 3 条要点 → 期待句 →
收束句 → 落款"。

所以这 15 分不是"写作文"，是**把 3 条中文要点译成 3–5 句英文，套进一个固定骨架**。
不需要构思，不需要观点，不需要高级词——只需要骨架熟、要点全、格式对。

三、逐年原题与范文
------------------------------------------------------------

■ 2017　投诉信
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】假如你是李明，最近参加了某旅行社（travel agency）组织的一次旅游，但你对此旅行很
不满意。请你向客服（customer service）写一封投诉信，阐述投诉的原因（如：酒店、饮食、交通
及费用等）并提出你期待的解决方法。

【范文】
Dear Customer Service,

I'm Li Ming, and I'm writing to express my dissatisfaction with the trip organized by your
travel agency last week.

To begin with, the hotel was far from what you had promised — the room was shabby and the hot
water was unreliable. Worse still, the meals were of poor quality and the itinerary was so
tight that we spent most of the time on the bus. Finally, we were charged extra fees not
mentioned in the contract.

I would like a full refund of the extra fees and a written explanation. I'm looking forward to
your prompt reply.

Yours,
Li Ming

【高分点】express my dissatisfaction with（投诉规范开头）· far from what you had promised
（"与承诺不符"）· Worse still（递进控诉）· a full refund（退款诉求）
【语体】克制坚定，不情绪化指责——这是投诉信的评分要点

■ 2018　邀请信（毕业晚会）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】假设你是李明，你们班将要举办一场毕业晚会，晚会节目丰富多彩，包括唱歌、跳舞、游戏
等。时间：5 月 28 日 19:30；地点：教学楼 502，届时班主任、任课老师和全班同学都来参加。
拟邀请外教 Robert 参加，请你写一封邀请信，邀请他出席晚会，欢迎他表演节目。

【范文】
Dear Robert,

How time flies! Our graduation ceremony party is just around the corner, and I'm writing on
behalf of our class to invite you to attend it.

The party will be held in Classroom 502, Teaching Building, at 7:30 p.m. on May 28. A variety
of wonderful programs have been prepared, such as singing, dancing and games. Our head
teacher, all the subject teachers and classmates will be present. We would be extremely
honored if you could come and even perform a program for us.

Looking forward to your coming!

Yours,
Li Ming

【高分点】How time flies（感叹式暖场）· on behalf of our class to invite you to（正式邀请）·
We would be honored if you could（虚拟语气收尾）

■ 2019　通知（招聘会）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】一家外资互联网企业将于 2019 年 3 月 11 日下午 2:30 在我校大礼堂举办招聘会，请你代
学生会写一份英文通知。要点：①招聘会参加人员 ②时间地点 ③需携带的资料与证件（身份证、个人
简历、英语应用能力考试合格证书等）。
参考词汇：招聘 recruitment；身份证 ID card；英语应用能力考试合格证书 the certificate of PRETCO

【范文】
Notice

A recruitment fair for a foreign-funded Internet company will be held in the School Auditorium
at 2:30 p.m. on March 11, 2019. All senior students who are interested are welcome to attend.

Candidates should bring your ID card, resume and the certificate of PRETCO. It is advisable to
dress formally and arrive ahead of time.

The Students' Union

【高分点】foreign-funded（外资的规范译法）· who are interested are welcome to attend（定语从句
收窄对象）· It is advisable to（建议的正式表达）

■ 2020　通知（讲座）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】我校图书馆将面向国际学生举办一场讲座，请你代图书馆写一份英文通知。要点：①时间
2020 年 6 月 15 日下午两点半到四点，地点三楼演讲厅 ②嘉宾王一博士，经济学教授 ③主题"摆地摊

参考词汇：演讲厅 Lecture Hall；经济学 economics；摆地摊 street vending

【范文】
Notice

A lecture will be held in the Lecture Hall on the third floor of our library from 2:30 p.m. to
4:00 p.m. on June 15, 2020. Dr. Wang Yi, a professor of economics, has been invited to deliver

All international students are welcome to attend. Please arrive ten minutes earlier and keep

The School Library

■ 2021　回复邮件（健身房咨询）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】假如你是李华，你班新来的留学生 Tom 想健身，发邮件询问你有关学校健身房的情况，请你
写一封电子邮件回复他。要点：①健身房的基本情况，如开放时间、位置、教练、培训等 ②预定和收费
情况。
参考词汇：gym；coach

【范文】
Dear Tom,

Welcome to our class! I'm more than happy to tell you something about our school gym.

Located next to the library, the gym opens from 6:00 a.m. to 10:00 p.m. every day. It is well
equipped with modern fitness equipment, and there are also professional coaches offering
training courses at different levels. As for booking, you can simply scan the QR code on the
gym's official account. When it comes to the charge, it is free for students, while a training
course costs 200 yuan per month.

I hope you will enjoy working out here!

Yours,
Li Hua

【高分点】Located next to（过去分词开头，简洁）· be well equipped with（设施齐全）· As for /
When it comes to（话题切换，咨询回复的骨架句式）

■ 2022　邀请邮件（端午主题班会）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】假如你是班长李华。端午将至，你班计划举办主题班会。请给 Mr. Smith 写一封电子邮件邀请
他参加。要点：①时间 6 月 3 日晚上 8 点 ②地点教学楼 306 教室 ③主要活动：包粽子、朗诵诗歌、
讲故事等。

【范文】
Dear Mr. Smith,

On behalf of our class, I'm writing to invite you to attend our themed class meeting on the
Dragon Boat Festival.

The meeting will be held in Classroom 306, Teaching Building, from 8:00 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. on
June 3. Various activities have been prepared, including making zongzi, reciting poems and

Li Hua

■ 2023　应聘邮件（中文家教）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】你是李华。Mr. Smith 要帮他女儿找一个中文家教。请给他写一封电子邮件应聘。要点：
①自我介绍 ②自荐理由 ③希望得到这份工作。
参考词汇：tutor

【范文】
Dear Mr. Smith,

I'm Li Hua, a senior from Guangdong University, and I'm writing to apply for the position of
Chinese tutor for your daughter.

I am confident that I am the right person for this job. To begin with, as a Chinese Literature
major, I have a good command of Chinese. Besides, I once tutored two international students in
Mandarin, which helped them make rapid progress. More importantly, I am patient and good with
kids.

I would really appreciate the opportunity to help your daughter. Looking forward to your reply.

Yours,
Li Hua

【高分点】apply for the position of（应聘规范说法）· To begin with / Besides / More
importantly（理由分层三连）· a good command of（"精通"的高级替换）
【判据】自荐理由最容易写成空话——正确姿势是"身份 + 证据"，每点都要有事实支撑，不能只堆形容词

■ 2024　邀请邮件（征文比赛）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】你是李华，你校英语报在为外国交换生开展名为"我眼中的中国"征文比赛，请你给交换生 Tim
写一封电子邮件，邀请他参赛。要点：①活动介绍 ②征文要求 ③邀请参赛。
参考词汇：writing contest

【范文】
Dear Tim,

How is everything going? I'm writing to tell you some good news. Our school English newspaper
is holding a writing contest themed "China in My Eyes" for international students, and I'd like
to invite you to take part.

As for the requirements, you are expected to write about 200 words sharing your experiences and
feelings about China — the food, the people or the culture that impresses you most. Please
submit your entry to englishpaper@school.edu.cn by June 10.

It's a great chance to share your story. Looking forward to your participation!

Yours,
Li Hua

【高分点】I'm writing to tell you some good news（告知类开头）· is holding a writing contest
themed（活动介绍的规范表达）· be expected to（要求的委婉说法）

【题目】假如你是学生会主席李华，原定于下周五举行的留学生茶话会改期。请你写一则英文通知。
要点：①对改期表示歉意 ②说明改期的具体原因 ③说明改期后的具体时间和地点。

We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause（致歉万能句）· Your understanding
is greatly appreciated（收束）
【版本说明】公众号另有一版改写范文（新时间写 December 26），与 PDF 文字层不一致。以 PDF 为准。

■ 2026　推荐邮件（朋友来访）
------------------------------------------------------------
【题目】假定你是李华，你的英国朋友迈克（Mike）正在北京留学，下周要来你的城市找你玩。请你给
他写一封电子邮件，内容包括：①表达欢迎 ②推荐一个旅游景点 ③推荐前往该景点的交通方式。
要求：词数 80—100 词，可适当增加细节；邮件开头和结尾已给出，不计入总词数。

【范文】
Dear Mike,

I'm more than delighted to hear that you're coming to my city for a two-day visit next week!

For your trip, I highly recommend the Ancient City Wall, which offers a stunning view of our
city's history. You can take Subway Line 1 there directly — it's fast and convenient. If time
permits, the City Museum is also worth a visit, displaying rich local cultural relics, and Bus
No. 5 stops right at its gate.

I can't wait to show you around and make your trip unforgettable.

Yours,
Li Hua

【高分点】more than delighted to（远比 happy 高级）· highly recommend · offers a stunning view
of · is worth a visit · I can't wait to
【判据】推荐类邮件是近年新动向——不再是校园内部事务，而是"向外国朋友介绍家乡"，落点在跨文化
交际。推荐景点要具体（名字 + 特色），交通方式要可操作（几号线 / 几路车）。

四、命题规律六条
------------------------------------------------------------
规律 1　**体裁封闭，就在三类里出题。** 十年全是应用文，集中在电子邮件（5 年）、通知（3 年）、
邀请信与投诉信（各 1 年）。复习时间按"书信 60% / 通知 40%"分配。

规律 2　**"李华"宇宙，十年九考。** 2021—2026 连续六年主角都是李华。命题人默认考生对"李华"这个
身份零理解成本，直接署名即可。

规律 3　**场景高度绑定校园与跨文化交流。** 留学生、外教、交换生、英国朋友——十年里有八年主角是
"外国人"。背熟"校园设施、传统节日、中国文化"三类词块，覆盖绝大多数场景。

规律 4　**三点式提纲，答案结构提前锁定。** 每年都是 3 个中文要点，直接对应"开头 2 句 + 主体 3 点
+ 结尾 1—2 句"的三段式。提纲即结构。

规律 5　**中国元素持续升温。** 端午节（2022）、"我眼中的中国"（2024）、向外国朋友推荐家乡景点
（2026）——"讲好中国故事"是明确风向。

规律 6　**词数与格式要求十年稳定。** 80—100 词；不得出现真实姓名与学校名称；题目给了开头结尾
则不计入总词数。每年都有考生因格式丢分（通知漏标题、书信漏署名）。

五、功能句库（30 句 / 6 组）
------------------------------------------------------------
■ 第 1 组　开篇目的
1. I'm writing to invite you to ...
2. I'm writing to apply for the position of ...
3. I'm writing to express my sincere gratitude for ...
4. I'm writing to apologize for ...
5. I'm writing to suggest that you (should) ...

■ 第 2 组　自我介绍与背景
6. I'm Li Hua, a 22-year-old student majoring in Chinese at a university in Guangzhou.
7. Knowing that you are interested in ..., I'm writing to ...
8. I'm more than happy to tell you something about ...
9. I'm glad to hear that ...
10. I'm sorry to hear that ...

■ 第 3 组　展开要点（连接词）
11. First and foremost, ...
12. Moreover, ...
13. In addition, ... / What's more, ...
14. Last but not least, ...
15. To begin with, ... Besides, ... More importantly, ...

■ 第 4 组　时间地点与细节
16. The activity will be held in ... from ... to ... on ...
17. It will start at 2:00 p.m. and last about three hours.
18. The activity aims to help us learn more about ...
19. All submissions must be received by ...
20. Those who are interested please sign up before ...

■ 第 5 组　期待与收束
21. I would be grateful if you could give me this opportunity.
22. We would be honored if you could come.
23. Why not participate in this activity?
24. I'm sure that you will have a good performance in it.
25. Looking forward to your reply.

■ 第 6 组　高分替换（加分项）
26. happy → I'm more than delighted to hear that ...
27. good/nice → It offers a stunning view of ... / is worth a visit
28. recommend → I highly recommend ... , which ...
29. 表原因 → Due to an unexpected scheduling conflict, ...
30. 表建议 → It is advisable to dress formally and arrive ahead of time.

**用法**：一篇 100 词的作文实际只用 6–8 句——开篇 1 句 + 连接词 2–3 个 + 收束 2 句。
先把第 1 组和第 5 组背死。

六、格式清单（写完对着打钩）
------------------------------------------------------------
【书信 / 邮件】
□ 称呼 Dear + 名 + 逗号（Dear Tom, 不是 Dear Tom:）
□ 第一段第一句是目的句（I'm writing to ...）
□ 正文分段，段与段之间空一行
□ 期待句 + 收束句各一句
□ 落款两行：Yours, / Li Hua（正式用 Yours sincerely,）
□ 署名必须与题目一致（假定你是李华就写 Li Hua）

【通知 NOTICE】
□ 居中大写标题 Notice
□ 没有 Dear XX 开头（或写 Dear international students,）
□ 正文第一句说明事由
□ 右下角：发布单位（The School Library / The Students' Union）+ 日期
□ 语气客观，不用第一人称感慨

七、常见失分点
------------------------------------------------------------
1. **漏要点** —— 权重最高的扣分项，3 条要点漏 1 条直接降档。养成逐条打钩的习惯。
2. **格式错** —— 通知漏标题、书信漏署名、称呼用冒号。这些不需要英语能力，纯白丢。
3. **词数失控** —— 低于 80 判内容不完整；高于 120 错误密度上升。写 90–110 最稳。
4. **时态混用** —— 描述将来活动用一般将来时（will be held），描述已发生用过去时，同一段里
   不能混。
5. **可数名词裸奔** —— a lecture / lectures，不能只写 lecture。
6. **不可数加 s** —— knowledge / information / advice / news 没有复数。
7. **中式表达** —— I very like it → I like it very much；open the light → turn on the light。
8. **出现真实姓名学校** —— 题目明确禁止，写了按违规处理。
