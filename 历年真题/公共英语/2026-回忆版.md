### Passage B（题 6–10）Rico，流浪狗变搜救明星

**1** Rico is a star pet detective, using his extraordinary sense of smell to reunite lost pets with their families. But before Rico became a "star", he was a poor homeless dog.

**2** The life-changing moment for Rico came when he met Jane, a warm-hearted dog trainer. It was a cold winter day. Rico was lying by the roadside, sick and hungry. Then Jane appeared in Rico's life. She took Rico home and gave him food and shelter. For the first time in his dog life, Rico knew what it was like to have a home.

**3** With Jane's great care, Rico became healthier and grew stronger. He loved to stay with Jane and followed her everywhere, even to her workplace—a dog training center. One day, when Jane was training other dogs on pet-finding skills, she tried some training on Rico. To Jane's surprise, Rico stood out from the other dogs for his amazing talent for tracking and sniffing out lost animals. In just a few months, Rico was aiding in the search for pets that had gone missing.

**4** Over the years, Rico has participated in more than 20 successful rescues. One of Rico's most unusual rescues involved a capybara (水豚) named Kiwi, which ran away from a zoo. When the zoo's usual method **fell short**, they sought help from Jane and Rico. By then Kiwi had disappeared for days and little fresh trail (足迹) was left. In spite of the challenge, Rico guided the rescue team to the right area miles away from the zoo, where Kiwi was found safe.

**5** Now, Jane and Rico are not only rescuing animals but also helping pet owners reconnect with their furry friends. The story of Rico is a moving reminder that kindness and a second chance can work miracles—not only to animals, but also to the families they help reunite.

**答案：6 A｜7 D｜8 A｜9 B｜10 D**

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 6 | **A** | 第 2 段 `Rico was lying by the roadside, sick and hungry` → 遇见 Jane 前是流浪狗。**B 是过度推断**（原文没说被遗弃），**C 是以偏概全**（只说 Jane 救了这一只），**D 因果颠倒**（Jane 本就是 dog trainer） |

### Passage C（题 11–15）旱作农业 Dry Farming

**1** The industrialization of agriculture has allowed us to produce larger quantities of crops but it has also led to a significant increase in water usage. Agriculture is now an increasingly popular option for farmers.

**2** Dry farming is a method of growing crops without irrigation in regions that receive low levels of rainfall. This type of farming relies on crops that are naturally adapted to dry conditions. Farmers usually plant seeds at wider intervals to reduce competition for water.

**3** Such farming practices can be traced back to thousands of years ago, when they were adopted in dry regions such as the Mediterranean, the Middle East and North Africa. It is believed that China has a history of more than 8,000 years in dry farming.

**4** This farming method has been widely popularized since the 19th century because it offers notable benefits. Firstly, it promotes sustainable agriculture as it requires significantly less water. Additionally, dry farming has been shown to produce crops with more concentrated flavors and nutrients. This is because the water stress encourages deeper root growth, leading to a stronger root system to take in nutrients more efficiently from the soil. Finally, adopting dry farming could also lower overall production costs, as it removes the need for expensive irrigation equipment and reduces energy consumption.

**5** Yet dry farming also comes with its own challenges. For example, farmers must be able to read the signs of soil and adapt their techniques to changing weather conditions. These challenges are related to how to practice dry farming effectively and appropriately. They suggest the need for dry farmers to constantly update their knowledge of agriculture and learning of farming techniques. Although there are such challenges, it is widely agreed that dry farming is a highly valuable alternative in drier environments.

**11. According to Paragraph 1, what has led to a significant increase in water usage?**
A. Modern industry　　B. Dry farming
C. Agriculture industrialization　　D. Crops production

**12. When practicing dry farming, farmers often ____.**
A. select certain crops　　B. compete for water
C. plant crops in a tiny space　　D. increase water consumption

**13. Which of the following is a benefit of dry farming?**
A. Raised level of rainfall
B. Decreased energy production
C. Crop with more concentrated nutrients
D. Farming with the latest irrigation system

**14. What do dry farmers need to do to overcome the challenges?**
A. To create driver engagement
B. To change weather condition
C. To advocate soil conservation
D. To update dry farming practices

**15. The passage is most probably written by ____.**
A. a gardener　　B. a researcher　　C. a historian　　D. a salesperson

**答案：11 C｜12 A｜13 C｜14 D｜15 B**

| 题 | 答案 | 解析 |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 11 | **C** | 第 1 段第 1 句 `The industrialization of agriculture … has also led to a significant increase in water usage`。**B 项正好反了**——旱作是「不用灌溉」 |
| 12 | **A** | 第 2 段 `relies on crops that are naturally adapted to dry conditions` + `plant seeds at wider intervals` → 选特定作物 |
| 13 | **C** | 第 4 段 `produce crops with more concentrated flavors and nutrients`。**B 项偷换概念**：原文 `reduces energy consumption`（减少能源消耗）≠ `decreased energy production`（降低能源产量） |
| 14 | **D** | 第 5 段 `constantly update their knowledge of agriculture and learning of farming techniques` |
| 15 | **B** | 全文有数据（8,000 年）、有机制解释（water stress → deeper root）、有客观结论，是**研究科普文风** |

> **第 13 题 B 项是全卷最精妙的陷阱**：`consumption`（消耗）和 `production`（产量）只差一个词，但意思完全相反。做细节题时**必须逐词比对，不能扫一眼就选**。

### Passage D（题 16–20）选句填空：为什么我们爱重看老电影

> 🆕 **重要更正**：本节是 **5 选 5**，原卷只给了 A–E 五个选项，**没有干扰项**。此前本库推断「应为 7 选 5，缺 2 个干扰项」是**错的**。

On some bad days when we are really stressed out, we just want to treat ourselves to a good movie. **__16__** Even though we've watched it a thousand times, we still feel life gets a little lighter while watching this old movie. Why so?

First, old movies can spare us the trouble of decision-making. With a variety of movies to choose from, we may spend hours on decision-making. **__17__**

Second, old movies can offer us comfort and predictability. We know exactly what is going to happen. **__18__** This can give us a sense of certainty, especially when our days are chaotic.

Besides, **__19__** old movies' content can bring back memories from happier, simpler times, like when we were kids.

When watching a new movie, we have to put in much effort, getting familiar with new characters and trying to figure out the storyline. But with one that we've already seen, there's no such pressure. That's exactly what we need when we want mindless entertainment. **__20__**

**选项**
- **A.** Of all the movies frees us from such trouble. ⚠️（原卷此处疑漏字，语法不完整，见下注）
- **B.** But going back to old movies frees us from such trouble.
- **C.** Finally, old movies allow us to relax with minimum effort.
- **D.** That's because old movies remind us of who we are when we first watched them.
- **E.** So, we won't be bothered even when there are shocking story-lines or unhappy endings.

**答案：16 A｜17 B｜18 E｜19 D｜20 C**

| 空 | 答案 | 逻辑依据 |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 16 | **A** | 承接 `we just want to treat ourselves to a good movie`，先说「电影能让我们摆脱烦恼」，再由下一句 `Even though … this old movie` 转到「老电影」 |
| 17 | **B** | `But` 转折承接 `spend hours on decision-making`，`such trouble` 指代「选择困难」 |
| 18 | **E** | 承接 `We know exactly what is going to happen` → 所以「即使有反转或悲剧结局也不会被影响」 |
| 19 | **D** | 空后 `old movies' content can bring back memories`，D 的 `remind us of who we are when we first watched them` 正好引出 |
| 20 | **C** | 段落总结 + `Finally` 呼应前文 First / Second / Besides 的并列结构，且 `minimum effort` 对应 `no such pressure` |

> ⚠️ **A 选项存疑**：原卷印作 `Of all the movies frees us from such trouble.`，缺少主语中心词，语法不通。按 B 项结构推测原文应为 `Of all the movies, [watching a familiar one] frees us from such trouble.` 之类。**不影响第 16 题选 A**，但引用原文时请注意这处疑点。
>
> 💡 **做题技巧**：本节 5 个空全在段尾或段中，**先看空后的连接词**（But / Even though / Besides / Finally），再回选项找对应逻辑词——第 17 题靠 `But`、第 20 题靠 `Finally`，两题可以「秒选」。

## 第三节 语法填空（本节共 10 小题，每小题 1.5 分，共 15 分）

Lily is a poet. But she seldom writes poetry with pen and ink. Every time she feels like creating a poem, she quickly **__36__** (open) the note-taking app on her cellphone and takes down her thoughts and feelings. This way, her ideas never get **__37__** (lose).

Some of the ideas might stay unfinished, but Lily isn't worried. She is not in a hurry to complete the poem right away **__38__** she can always revisit the unfinished pieces some time later. She is able to polish her poetry anytime and anywhere **__39__** the help of her cellphone.

When a poem feels finished to **__40__** (she), Lily often adds visual elements to the words. Sometimes she uses photos from her phone. Other times, she uses design apps to **__41__** (add) a picture to her poem, extending poetry writing to visual art.

Lily often shares her poems on social media platforms. For her, poetry sharing is more than **__42__** social activity. She performs some of her **__43__** (poem) at a local cafe, and her audience is **__44__** (usual) her trusted friends and family members. When **__45__** (perform), she always turns off the lights and asks everyone to turn on his cellphone torch, creating a moment of warmth. In this way, Lily turns poetry sharing into a performing art.

| 题 | 答案 | 考点 |
|:---:|:---:|:---|
| 36 | **opens** | 主谓一致。主语 she，一般现在时第三人称单数 |
| 37 | **lost** | 过去分词作表语。`get lost` = 丢失（系表结构） |
| 38 | **because** | 无提示词→填连词。**原因**状语从句：后句解释「为什么不用急着完成」——因为她随时可以回头改 |
| 39 | **with** | 无提示词→填介词。`with the help of` 固定搭配 |
| 40 | **her** | 人称代词宾格。介词 to 后用宾格 |
| 41 | **To add** | 不定式表**目的**。`use sth to do sth`，此处填 `to add`（用设计软件**是为了**加图） |
| 42 | **a** | 冠词。`a social activity` 泛指一次社交活动 |
| 43 | **poems** | 名词复数。`some of her poems` |
| 44 | **usually** | 副词。修饰系动词 is 后的表语，用副词 |
| 45 | **performing** | 现在分词。`When performing` = When she performs，状语从句省略 |

> 💡 **语法填空 10 空的规律**：**3 空无提示词**（38 连词、39 介词、42 冠词）+ **7 空有提示词**（时态/非谓语/词性转换）。**无提示词的三空是固定套路**：先判断缺连词、介词还是冠词，这是最容易拿分的 4.5 分。
