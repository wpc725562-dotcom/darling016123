## 2023 阅读 B · 说明文　上海第一座森林图书馆

- **来源**：历年真题/公共英语/2023.md · 第 6–10 题
- **规格**：阅读理解 Passage B · 5 题 × 2 分 = 10 分 · 限时 12 分钟

- **答案**：6.B　7.A　8.B　9.A　10.C

### 原文与译文

**[1]**

> The first forest library in Shanghai recently **opened** in public. Known as Read & Joy Forest, the library was jointly founded by Pudong New Area and Shanghai Library. It is **located** in an urban wood, covering an area of 20,546 square meters.

上海第一座森林图书馆最近对外开放，名叫「悦读森林」，由浦东新区与上海图书馆共建，坐落于一片城市林地中，占地 20,546 平方米。

**[2]**

> The **design** of forest library **allows** an environment-friendly **principle** — **ensuring** minimum disturbance to **nature**, with **existing** green spaces **connected** by wooden walkways.

图书馆的设计遵循一条环保原则 —— 把对自然的干扰降到最低，用木栈道把现有绿地连起来。

**[3]**

> The library is in the **open** air and equipped with facilities such as benches and an **information** booth. Visitors can read and relax in a natural **environment**. They can take a pleasant walk in the quiet and verdant forest, which **includes** Camphor, Cedar and Osmanthus trees.

图书馆是露天的，配有长椅、问询处等设施。读者能在自然环境中阅读放松，也可以在幽静葱郁的林子里惬意散步，林中有香樟、雪松和桂花树。

**[4]**

> "I learnt about the forest library from my friends, so I came here," said a 21-year-old college student, Mr. Wang. "The green outdoor **environment** helps **reduce** eyestrain. I will come back for **sure**."

21 岁的大学生王先生说：「我是听朋友说起才来的。绿色的户外环境能缓解视疲劳，我肯定还会再来。」

**[5]**

> Having a library in the woods is an extension of reading indoors, according to Xu Qiang, who works in Shanghai Library. "It **offers** a **different** reading **experience** to readers," he added.

上海图书馆的徐强认为，把图书馆开进树林是室内阅读的一种延伸，「它给读者带来了不一样的阅读体验」。

**[6]**

> Most of the visitors to the forest library are young people and parents with kids. They are really **impressed** by the fresh air and the green. Since most of the time they stay indoors, this special library makes it possible for them to get close to **nature** surroundings. They feel really relaxed there.

访客大多是年轻人和带孩子的家长，新鲜的空气和满眼绿色让他们印象很深。因为平时多数时间待在室内，这座特别的图书馆让他们有机会亲近自然，感到格外放松。

### 方法提示

**第 6 题是「复现词陷阱」。**原文 existing green spaces 里的 green spaces 被搬进干扰项 A，但原文说的是「现有的」绿地，不是「增加」绿地。同一天完形 32 题也踩过这个坑。

### 错题与判据

- **6** 答 A increasing green spaces → **B** making the least change to nature：minimum disturbance = least change；A 的 green spaces 是复现词诱饵，原文是 *existing*（现有的）不是 increasing。
- 判据：复现词 ≠ 答案，要逐词核对它对应的是不是题干问的那个东西。

## 2023 阅读 C · 议论文　把学校变得像幼儿园

- **来源**：历年真题/公共英语/2023.md · 第 11–15 题
- **规格**：阅读理解 Passage C · 5 题 × 2 分 = 10 分 · 限时 18 分钟
- **成绩**：2026-11-04 3/5（12 题未答）（最近：3/5）
- **答案**：11.C　12.C　13.A　14.D　15.B

### 原文与译文

**[1]**

> Since the first kindergarten **opened** in 1837, kindergarten has been a place for telling stories, **building** castles, drawing pictures, and learning to share. But today, children are **spending** more time in kindergartens working out math problems and memorizing words. In **short**, kindergartens are becoming more like schools.

自 1837 年第一所幼儿园开办以来，幼儿园一直是讲故事、搭城堡、画画、学分享的地方。可如今孩子们在园里花更多时间算数学、背单词 —— 简言之，幼儿园正变得越来越像学校。

**[2]**

> In my **mind**, exactly the opposite is needed. Instead of making kindergartens like schools, we need to make schools more like kindergartens.

**【划线句】**在我看来，恰恰需要反过来：不是把幼儿园办成学校，而是把学校办得更像幼儿园。

**[3]**

> As I see it, the kindergarten **approach** to learning is ideally suited to the core need of the 21st century — the **ability** to think creatively. Unfortunately, most schools are out of step with today's need: they are not **designed** to help students **develop** as creative thinkers. But kindergartens, at least those that **remain** **true** to the kindergarten tradition, do things **differently**.

我认为，幼儿园式的学习方式恰好契合 21 世纪的核心需求 —— 创造性思考的能力。可惜大多数学校跟这个需求脱了节：它们不是为培养创造性思考者设计的。而幼儿园，至少那些守住了幼儿园传统的，做法完全不同。

**[4]**

> What do I mean by the kindergarten **approach** to learning? In traditional kindergartens, children are **constantly** **designing**, creating, experimenting, and **exploring**. Two children are **building** towers with wooden blocks. A classmate sees the towers and starts pushing his toy car between them. But the towers are too close together, so the children start moving the towers further apart to make room for the car. In the **process**, one of the towers falls down. After a brief argument over who is at fault, they start talking about how to **build** a taller and stronger tower. Seeing this, the teacher shows them pictures of real world — tall buildings, and the children **notice** that the bottoms of the buildings are wider than the tops. So they **decide** to rebuild their block tower with a wider base than before.

我说的「幼儿园式学习」指什么？传统幼儿园里，孩子们不断设计、创造、试验、探索。两个孩子用积木搭塔，另一个同学看见了，把玩具车从两塔之间推过去；塔挨得太近，孩子们就把塔挪开来给车让路，过程中一座塔倒了。为谁的责任短暂争执后，他们开始讨论怎样搭得更高更牢。老师见状给他们看真实世界的图片 —— 高楼，孩子们注意到楼底比楼顶宽，于是决定把积木塔的底座加宽重建。

**[5]**

> This type of **process** is repeated over and over in kindergarten. The materials **vary** and the creations **vary**, but the core **process** is the same. In going through this **process**, kindergarten children **develop** and **improve** their **abilities** as creative thinkers. They learn to **develop** their own ideas, try them out, test the boundaries, experiment with alternatives, get input from others — and, perhaps most **significantly**, generate new ideas based on their **experiences**.

这样的过程在幼儿园里一遍遍重演：材料不同、作品不同，核心过程一样。走完这个过程，孩子的创造性思维能力得到发展：形成自己的想法、试出来、试探边界、换方案尝试、听取别人意见 —— 而最重要的，也许是能够基于自身经验生成新想法。

**[6]**

> The kindergarten **approach** to learning is well-matched to the core need of the current society, and should be **extended** to learners of all ages.

幼儿园式的学习方式与当今社会的核心需求高度契合，应当推广到所有年龄段的学习者。

### 方法提示

**主旨题要跳出来看首尾。**第 12 题（划线句）与第 15 题（写作目的）考的是同一个观点：该改的是学校，不是幼儿园。中间那段搭积木是演示，不是文章目的。

### 错题与判据

- **15** 答 D「改革传统幼儿园」→ **B**「推广幼儿园式方法」：把对象搞反了，作者要改的是学校。
- **12** 未答（聊天里丢了下划线标记，已补原句）：与 15 同一观点，答案 C。
- 判据：主旨题看转折句（第 2 段）+ 末段结论（should be extended），中间长例子只作演示。

比如你会查到，这个短语早在 1970 年、互联网发明之前就已被使用。但其中大量信息无关紧要、毫无用处。

**[3]**

> Every day, there are news and sports websites to watch, emails that need to be answered, and people who want to chat with you online. Back in the real world, friends, family, and colleagues also have things to tell you.

每天都有新闻和体育网站要看、邮件要回、有人想在网上找你聊；回到现实世界，朋友、家人、同事也都有话要跟你说。

**[5]**

> **Dealing** with the overloaded **information**, they sit in front of their computers for hours before they know it. So they cannot find time to do exercise or get enough time for **quality** sleep.

为了处理过载的信息，他们不知不觉就在电脑前坐了好几个小时，于是既挤不出时间运动，也睡不够高质量觉。

**[6]**

## 2023 语法填空　来到这里的七个月

- **来源**：历年真题/公共英语/2023.md · 第 36–45 题
- **规格**：语法填空 · 10 空 × 1.5 分 = 15 分 · 限时 12 分钟
- **成绩**：2026-11-04 3/10（最近：3/10）
- **答案**：36.have changed　37.excited　38.to live　39.gradually　40.powerful　41.nights　42.spending　43.it　44.a　45.that/which

### 原文与译文

**[1]**

> It's been seven months since I arrived here and things ___ (**change**) so much in such a **short** period of time.

我到这儿已经七个月了，这么短的时间里，一切都变了很多。（since + 时间段 → 现在完成 have changed）

**[2]**

> When I first arrived, I was ___ (excite) and **nervous** to start a new adventure in a country I love. The first few days seemed so long ago. I was trying to find a place ___ (live) in, **organizing** my timetable with the schools, and meeting new people.

刚到时，在这个我热爱的国家开启新旅程，我既兴奋又紧张。最初那几天仿佛很久以前。我忙着找住处、跟学校排课表、认识新朋友。（人感到 → excited；句中已有 was trying，第二个动词用非谓语 to live）

> After a couple of weeks, however, the excitement started to fade, and homesickness ___ (gradual) set in. It wasn't a ___ (power) homesickness but enough to make me **spend** more than a few ___ (night) thinking about family and friends back home, wondering if I should be there with them.

**[4]**

I **suppose** we should never forget how important ___ is to have good people in our life, because they can make such ___ **difference**.

交到新朋友、跟他们一起练语言、吃饭、结伴逛新地方之后，这一切才开始改变。我想我们永远不该忘记，生命里有好人是多重要的一件事，因为他们能带来如此大的不同。（and 前是 making → spending；形式主语 → it；make a difference 固定搭配 → a）

### 方法提示

**错因不是词汇，是没先判「有提示 / 无提示」和词性。**7 个错里 4 个是直接变形甚至造词（nightful 不是英语单词）。四步法：通读抓时态基调 → 标有/无 → 先定词性再定形式 → 纯空格看位置。

### 错题与判据

- **36** changed → have changed：没抓全文时态基调，since 是现在完成的硬标志。
- **37** exciting → excited：修饰人用 -ed，修饰物用 -ing。
- **38** lives → to live：句中已有谓语，多出来的动词变非谓语；一个简单句只有一个谓语。
- **40** powered → powerful：a + ___ + 名词，位置决定只能是形容词。
- **41** nightful → nights：**造词了**。括号给了词，答案只能是它的某种形式；a few 后接复数。
- **43** that → it：how + 形 + ___ is + to do 是形式主语句。

DNA left behind like this is called "environmental" DNA, or eDNA. Scientists can use this eDNA to tell what kinds of animals are in a **certain** place.

每种生物都有可用于识别身份的 DNA。所有生物走到哪儿都会留下一点 DNA —— 皮屑、毛发、羽毛或排泄物。这样留下的 DNA 叫「环境 DNA」，即 eDNA。科学家可以用它判断某个地方有哪些动物。

**[4]**

> Testing for eDNA isn't a new idea, but most of the time, scientists look for it in water. DNA in the air is usually so small that it would take a microscope to see it.

检测 eDNA 不算新思路，但多数时候科学家是在水里找它。空气里的 DNA 通常小到要用显微镜才看得见。

**[5]**

> The Danish scientists worked on an experiment. They collected samples from **different** locations at a zoo, using vacuums and fans to collect extremely tiny bits of DNA onto very high **quality** filters. In the laboratory, they got the DNA from the filters and made copies of it to study. By **comparing** these samples with **examples** of DNA from **different** animals, the scientists were **able** to **identify** many **different** animals at the zoo. They **identified** 49 **different** kinds of animals. They even **identified** DNA from animals that were inside sealed buildings.

丹麦科学家做了个实验：在动物园不同地点采样，用吸尘器和风扇把极微小的 DNA 收集到高质量滤膜上；在实验室里把 DNA 从滤膜上取出来、复制后研究。把这些样本与已知动物的 DNA 比对，他们成功识别出园里许多动物，共 49 种，甚至连封闭馆舍内动物的 DNA 也测到了。

**[6]**

> The scientists chose to test in the zoo because there were rare animals not naturally found in the area. As Dr. Elizabeth Clare, who led the team, said, "There's no other **way** I would **detect** DNA from a tiger, except for the zoo's tiger."

选在动物园做实验，是因为那里有本地自然界找不到的珍稀动物。带队的是伊丽莎白·克莱尔博士，她说：「除了动物园的老虎，我没有别的途径能测到老虎的 DNA。」

**[7]**

> The researchers are excited about the **ways** this new **method** could be used in the wild. Scientists have been looking for better **ways** to track endangered animals without interfering with them. If scientists know where animals live, they can do a better job of **protecting** them.

研究者对这个方法在野外的使用前景很兴奋。科学家一直在找更好的办法，既能追踪濒危动物又不打扰它们。只要知道动物住在哪里，保护工作就能做得更好。

### 方法提示

**30 秒判断刚答对，整篇做题仍 2/5 —— R7「判断对 ≠ 做对」的实证。**三个错题共同点：定位没走完最后一步。Q11 是定位句抄对了却没换词；Q13/Q14 是压根没回原文，用整体印象或常识补答案。⇒ 新口径：细节题「找」到之后，必须**把那句话逐字对到某一个选项**，对不上就换词，不许凭印象补。D032 复检必须用未做过的整篇阅读，不能拿选择判断顶替。

### 错题与判据

- **11** 定位句 keep track of animals that are hard to spot 抄对了，却选 B identify rare animal species → **A To trace hard-to-spot animals**：只差把 keep track of 换成 trace，结果被 including endangered animals 生出的 rare species 诱饵带走。
- **13** 选 A from air → **D in water**：原文 most of the time, scientists look for it in water，题眼 In most cases 就是 most of the time 的换说。原词复现题反而错，根因是拿「新方法从空气吸 DNA」的整体印象代替回原文定位。
- **14** 选 B easily tested → **A samples not locally found**：原文 because there were rare animals not naturally found in the area。「动物容易测」听着合理，文章里根本没这句，是凭常识补的。
- ✅ **12** didn't have high hopes → doubtful：改写识别成功（发题时把四个态度词全给了中文，不偏向任何一个）。✅ **15** 判 Science：先判题型的提醒接住了。

## 2022 七选五　从默片到有声片

- **来源**：历年真题/公共英语/2022.md · 第 16–20 题
- **规格**：七选五 · 5 题 × 2 分 = 10 分 · 限时 12 分钟
- **成绩**：2026-11-06 3/5（最近：3/5）
- **答案**：16.B　17.C　18.D　19.E　20.A

### 原文与译文

**[1]**

> When people go to the movies today, they can **settle** in to watch and listen to a story. But what if when the lights dimmed and the movie began, there was no dialogue, sound **effects**, or music? <b>16</b> ______ Those silent films are important to film history.

今天人们进电影院，可以安坐下来看、听一个故事。可要是灯一暗、电影开始，却没有对白、没有音效、也没有音乐呢？**16** ______ 那些默片在电影史上很重要。

**[2]**

> When movie theaters showed silent films, a musician was often there to play live music along with the movie. <b>17</b> ______ Occasionally, musicians or theater staff also **produced** sound **effects**, such as tires screeching or doors slamming.

放映默片时，影院常有一位乐师现场配乐。**17** ______ 偶尔，乐师或影院工作人员还会制造音效，比如轮胎尖叫、摔门声。

**[3]**

> <b>18</b> ______ Instead, the story was told through the performers' motions and through words shown on the screen.

**18** ______ 相反，故事是靠演员的动作和打在银幕上的字来讲的。

**[4]**

> When movies first **included** sound, audiences weren't **sure** what to think. Not everyone was excited about the new type of film which became known as the "talkie". <b>19</b> ______ Clara Bow, who was a **famous** silent-movie actress in the early 1920s, was too **nervous** about her voice to become a star in the world of talking pictures. She faded from the spotlight and left show business altogether.

电影刚有声音时，观众拿不准该怎么看。并不是人人都对这种后来叫「有声片」的新片兴奋。**19** ______ 克拉拉·鲍是 20 年代早期著名的默片演员，她对自己的声音太紧张，没能在有声片世界里当上明星，最终淡出公众视线、彻底离开演艺圈。

**[5]**

> The first movie with sound, The Jazz Singer, was **released** in 1927. <b>20</b> ______ After that, talking pictures became a huge **success**.

第一部有声片《爵士歌王》1927 年上映。**20** ______ 此后，有声片大获成功。

**[6]**

> A. It marked the beginning of a new era.　B. That's what the first movies were like.　C. Music was chosen to **fit** the mood of the movie.　D. However, there was no sound in the movie itself.　E. Many silent film performers had **trouble** with the new format.

A. 它标志着一个新时代的开端。　B. 最早的电影就是这个样子。　C. 配乐是按影片的情绪挑的。　D. 然而，影片本身并没有声音。　E. 许多默片演员适应不了这种新形式。

### 方法提示

**第 3 层「句间关系」。**这层不靠某一句，靠句子之间的关系：转折（Instead / However）、总括-举例、代词回指、顺承。用户回 B C E D A，标准是 B C D E A，**D/E 两空正好对调**，多半是排除到剩两项后凭印象填。⚠️ 本地卷面只抽出 A–E 五项，是 PDF 抽取丢了 F/G；四卷题头写「七选五」，**按 7 选 5 备考、保留排除法**。

### 错题与判据

- **18** 放 E → **D**：空后是 Instead, the story was told through...，前面必须先有一个被否定的命题。D 用 However 纠正「有现场音乐 ≠ 影片本身有声音」，是**连接词方向**线索。
- **19** 放 D → **E**：空后是 Clara Bow 这个具体演员的例子，空前应是复数总括句 Many silent film performers...，属**位置角色：段首总括 + 后接例证**。注意 new format 指刚出现的有声片，不是默片。
- 两空恰好互换，疑似排除到剩两项后凭印象填，与 C 篇 Q13/Q14「凭印象补」同族。
- ✅ 对题落在代词回指与顺承：16 用 That's 回指上句无声的设问、17 接现场音乐、20 接 After that。⇒ 五类线索里前两类能用，缺的是连接词方向与位置角色。
- 判据：先看空前后有没有指代（that / they / such）或方向词（Instead / However / After that），这一步优先于通读全文。
