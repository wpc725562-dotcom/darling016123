## 第一部分：阅读理解（共15小题，每小题2分，满分30分）

### Passage A

**Organic EcoPark** — At this all-in-one family fun destination, buying an admission ticket will allow you to choose from a wide range of activities, such as playing on a bouncy castle, riding horses, feeding fish, picking vegetables and mushrooms, or taking part in a workshop. ($12/person, Tel: 5570221)

**Go Green Farm** — At this farm, lots of the activities are mango-centric. You can get an insight into how the fruit is grown and how to use it to make drinks and cakes. You can even plant a mango tree. It is pet-friendly. ($10/person, Tel: 5579217)

**Sunrise Village** — At this more than 200-year-old village, which is home to one of the largest rice production areas in the city, you will get a chance to know the entire rice production process. (Free, Tel: 5545207)

**Butterfly Valley** — The farm is home to 80% of the city's butterfly species. You can sign up for painting classes, guided tours, and handicraft workshops. The farm doesn't take walk-ins, so you need to make a reservation. ($20/adults, $15/kids, Tel: 5583511)

**Holiday Farm** — If you're looking for unusual but fun things to do with the family, Holiday Farm will set you up with interesting workshops, camping experiences and parent-child activities. ($30/adults, $20/kids, Tel: 5568402)

**1. At Organic EcoPark, visitors can ______.**
A. build a castle &emsp; B. ride horses &emsp; C. plant vegetables &emsp; D. make drinks

**2. Which of the following suits people who have a dog?**
A. Go Green Farm. &emsp; B. Sunrise Village. &emsp; C. Butterfly Valley. &emsp; D. Holiday Farm.

**3. What makes the trip to Sunrise Village special?**
A. A guided tour. &emsp; B. Making butterfly handicrafts. &emsp; C. A camping experience. &emsp; D. Learning about the rice production.

**4. What do visitors need to do before going to Butterfly Valley?**
A. Study butterflies. &emsp; B. Take kids together. &emsp; C. Book in advance. &emsp; D. Sign up for classes.

**5. How much does it cost a parent with two kids to visit Holiday Farm?**
A. $50. &emsp; B. $70. &emsp; C. $80. &emsp; D. $100.

### Passage B

Philip Hayden, a primary school teacher, found a common weakness in some classrooms. They didn't have windows. And he felt it was affecting his students...

**6. How does the lack of windows affect students?**
A. It relieves their anxiety. &emsp; B. It boosts their creativity. &emsp; C. It shortens their attention span. &emsp; D. It regulates their behavior.
