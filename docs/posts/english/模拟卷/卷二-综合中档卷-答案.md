### 参考范文

Dear Li Ming,

I was sorry to hear that you've been feeling anxious because of the heavy workload. I understand how stressful it can be, and I want you to know that I'm here for you.

Here are some suggestions that might help. First, try to take regular breaks during your workday. Even a short walk can help clear your mind and reduce stress. Second, don't hesitate to talk to your colleagues or manager about your concerns. Sometimes sharing your feelings can make a big difference.

Remember, you don't have to face this alone. If you need someone to talk to, I'm always available. Let's meet up this weekend and have a proper chat.

Best wishes,
Zhang Wei
