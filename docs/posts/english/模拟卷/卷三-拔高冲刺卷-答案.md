## 第五部分：写作（15 分）

### 参考范文

**Should College Students Take Part-time Jobs?**

Nowadays, it is common for college students to take part-time jobs during their spare time. Some work as tutors, some as waiters, and others as interns in companies. This phenomenon has sparked heated debate.

On the one hand, part-time jobs offer several benefits. They provide students with valuable work experience and help them develop practical skills that cannot be learned in classrooms. Moreover, earning their own money can give students a sense of independence and responsibility.

On the other hand, there are also drawbacks. Part-time jobs may take up too much time and energy, distracting students from their studies. Some students even find their academic performance declining because they spend too much time working.

In my opinion, taking part-time jobs is beneficial as long as students maintain a proper balance between work and study. A part-time job of no more than 15 hours per week can enrich one's college experience without compromising academic performance. The key is to prioritize studies while using part-time work as a supplement to, not a replacement for, learning.
