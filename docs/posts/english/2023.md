### Passage A

**Power Station Gym** — newly-built swimming pool, latest fitness equipment, skilled trainers. (Closed on Sundays)

**Zest-booster Leisure Center** — open 24 hours, 400m running track, VIP programs. (Closed on Mondays)

**Wonder Ladies' Club** — female only, one-to-one training, healthcare lectures, member gatherings.

**Josh's Running Club** — organized by Josh Smith, a professional athlete. 5km run every Tuesday & Friday, 9pm. Free.

**Papa and Mamma's Health and Fitness** — for people above 60, yoga, dancing, free programs for non-members every Monday morning.

**1. At Power Station Gym, you can ______.**
