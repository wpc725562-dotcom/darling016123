requirements. Personal connections are not supposed to matter under the rule of law.

This is not to say that personal contacts, wealth, and social influence do not matter in situations where

In reality, connections can sometimes help a person get a government job. Rich people can sometimes go

law prevails, and Americans are proud that it does.
