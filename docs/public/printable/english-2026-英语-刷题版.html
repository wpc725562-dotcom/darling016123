Passage A（第 1–5 题）

原文：

Many people love to visit special cafes when traveling. A new kind of theme cafe has become popular recently. It combines entertainment, culture and daily services, attracting tourists from all over the world.
One famous cafe in Paris is called The Soul of Paris. It not only serves coffees and snacks but also offers a beautiful view of the whole city. Visitors can enjoy their drinks while looking out at the old buildings and streets.
Another special cafe is different from others. It provides a clothes-cleaning service for customers. While people are waiting for their clothes to be cleaned, they can have coffee and relax. This creative way has won many people's hearts.
There is also a bird-themed cafe. In the evening, at around 8 pm, many lovely birds fly around and stay with visitors. People can get close to the birds and take photos. The whole environment looks like a cartoon world, making people feel warm and happy.
These theme cafes bring new experiences to customers. They are not just places to drink coffee; they are cultural and fun spaces that make traveling more enjoyable.

1. Who will be most interested in these theme cafes?

  • A. A history lover.
  • B. A sports fan.
  • C. A businessman.
  • D. A driver.

答案与解析

答案：A

画面（帧 p01_0010–p01_0012）只对题干作了标注（箭头指向 these theme cafes），四个选项均无勾选；但逐字稿明说答案：

「……只有一个 culture 和这个历史，文化和历史能够对应起来，第一题选择 A 选项。」

逐字稿还逐项排除了另三项：B（sport 突出运动，文中没有运动相关）、C（商人，未提可供洽谈的场所）、D（司机，无相关因素）。

与据正文推断的结果一致：首段定位句 *It combines entertainment, culture and daily services* —— 主打「文化」的主题咖啡馆，最可能吸引 A. 历史爱好者。

2. Which cafe can offer a city view?

  • A. The cartoon cafe.
  • B. The bird cafe.
  • C. The Soul of Paris.
  • D. The clothes-cleaning cafe.

答案与解析

答案：C

定位句：*One famous cafe in Paris is called The Soul of Paris. It not only serves coffees and snacks but also offers a beautiful view of the whole city.*

「city view」对应「view of the whole city」，属同义替换题。

3. What is special about the second cafe mentioned?

  • A. It serves free coffee.
  • B. It can get customers' clothes clean.
  • C. It opens late at night.
  • D. It sells old clothes.

答案与解析

答案：B

定位句：*It provides a clothes-cleaning service for customers.*

「get clothes clean」= 「clothes-cleaning service」，同义替换。

4. When can people play with birds in the bird-themed cafe?

  • A. At 6 am.
  • B. At noon.
  • C. At 5 pm.
  • D. At 8 pm.

答案与解析

答案：D

定位句：*In the evening, at around 8 pm, many lovely birds fly around and stay with visitors.*

5. What makes the bird cafe special?

  • A. Its low price.
  • B. Its fast service.
  • C. Its comic-like environment.
  • D. Its large space.

答案与解析

答案：C

定位句：*The whole environment looks like a cartoon world, making people feel warm and happy.*

「comic-like」= 「like a cartoon world」，同义替换。

Passage B（第 6–10 题）

原文：

Rico is a star pet detective, using his extraordinary sense of smell to reunite lost pets with their families. But before Rico became a "star", he was a poor homeless dog.
The life-changing moment for Rico came when he met Jane, a warm-hearted dog trainer. It was a cold winter day. Rico was lying by the roadside, sick and hungry. Then Jane appeared in Rico's life. He took Rico home and gave him food and shelter. For the first time in his dog life, Rico knew what it was like to have a home.
With Jane's great care, Rico became healthier and grew stronger. He loved to stay with Jane and followed him everywhere, even to his workplace — a dog training center. One day, when Jane was training other dogs in pet-finding skills, he tried some training on Rico. To Jane's surprise, Rico stood out from the other dogs for his amazing talent for tracking and sniffing (嗅) out lost animals. In just a few months, Rico was aiding in the search for pets that had gone missing.
Over the years, Rico has participated in more than 20 successful rescues. One of Rico's most unusual rescues involved a capybara (水豚) named Kiwi, which ran away from a zoo. When the zoo's usual method fell short, they sought help from Jane and Rico. By then Kiwi had disappeared for days and little fresh trail (嗅迹) was left. In spite of the challenge, Rico guided the rescue team to the right area miles away from the zoo, where Kiwi was found safe.
Now, Jane and Rico are not only rescuing animals but also helping pet owners reconnect with their furry friends. The story of Rico is a moving reminder that kindness and a second chance can work miracles — not only for animals, but also for the families they help reunite.

6. What can we infer from paragraph 2?

  • A. Rico was a street dog before he met Jane.
  • B. Rico was abandoned by his former owner.
  • C. Jane often gives food and shelter to poor animals.
  • D. Jane became a dog trainer after he took Rico home.

答案与解析

答案：A（画面打勾 ✓）

第二段说 Rico 此前是「a poor homeless dog」，在路边「sick and hungry」，被 Jane 捡回家 —— 可推断 A。

B 错在「被前主人遗弃」文中未提；C 把个案泛化为「Jane 经常喂流浪动物」；D 因果颠倒（Jane 本来就是训犬师）。

  • A. identifying different objects
  • B. working with other trained dogs
  • C. finding the way to his owner's workplace
  • D. tracking lost pets through his sense of smell

答案与解析

答案：D（画面箭头指向 D）

定位句：*Rico stood out from the other dogs for his amazing talent for tracking and sniffing out lost animals*。

D 项是同义改写。

8. What does the underlined phrase "fell short" in paragraph 4 probably mean?

  • A. failed

  • D. were followed

答案与解析

答案：A（画面打勾 ✓）

上下文：*When the zoo's usual method fell short, they sought help from Jane and Rico.* —— 正因为它「不奏效」才会去求助，故 fell short = failed（未能达到预期）。

9. Which is the best word to describe Rico according to the passage?

  • A. Loyal.
  • B. Helpful.
  • C. Strong.
  • D. Healthy.

答案与解析

答案：B（画面箭头指向 B）

全文主线是 Rico 用嗅觉帮助找回走失宠物（20 多次成功救援），「Helpful」最贴合主旨。C（Strong）、D（Healthy）只是第二段描述其身体恢复的细节，A（Loyal）文中未突出。

10. What is the best title for this passage?

  • A. Training Dog on Lost Pets Finding
  • B. Dog Successfully Rescues Capybara
  • C. Bond Between Dog and His Trainer
  • D. Rescued Dog Becomes Star Detective

Passage C（第 11–15 题）

原文（主题：dry farming 旱作农业）：

…popular option for farmers.
Dry farming is a method of growing crops without irrigation (灌溉) in regions that receive low levels of rainfall. This type of farming relies on crops that are naturally adapted to dry conditions. Farmers usually plant seeds at wider intervals (间隔) to reduce competition for water.
Such farming practices can be traced back to thousands of years ago, when they were adopted in dry regions such as the Mediterranean, the Middle East and North Africa. It is believed that China has a history of more than 8,000 years of dry farming.
This farming method has been widely popularized since the 19th century because it offers notable benefits. Firstly, it promotes sustainable agriculture as it requires significantly less water. Additionally, dry farming has been shown to produce crops with more concentrated flavors and nutrients (营养成分). This is because the reduced water stress encourages deeper root growth, leading to a stronger root system to take in nutrients more efficiently from the soil. Finally, adopting dry farming could also lower overall production costs, as it removes the need for expensive irrigation equipment and reduces energy consumption.
Yet dry farming also comes with its own challenges. For example, farmers must be able to read the signs of soil and adapt their techniques to changing weather conditions. These challenges are related to how to practice dry farming effectively and appropriately. They suggest the need for dry farmers to constantly update their knowledge of agriculture and learning of farming techniques. Although there are such challenges, it is widely agreed that dry farming is a highly valuable alternative in drier environments.

11. According to Paragraph 1, what has led to a significant increase in water usage?

  • A. Modern industry.
  • B. Dry farming.
  • C. Agriculture industrialization.
  • D. Crops production.

答案与解析

答案：C（画面打勾 ✓）

首段：*The industrialization of agriculture has allowed us to produce larger quantities of crops but it has also led to a significant increase in water usage.*

主语是「农业的工业化」，即 C。

12. When practicing dry farming, farmers often ______.

  • A. select certain crops
  • B. compete for water
  • C. plant crops in a big space
  • D. increase water consumption

答案与解析

答案：A（画面打勾 ✓）

定位句：*This type of farming relies on crops that are naturally adapted to dry conditions* —— 即「选特定的作物」。

13. Which of the following is a benefit of dry farming?

  • A. Raised level of rainfall.
  • B. Decreased energy production.
  • C. Crop with more concentrated nutrients.
  • D. Farming with the latest irrigation system.

答案与解析

答案：C

定位句：*dry farming has been shown to produce crops with more concentrated flavors and nutrients*。

B 项是干扰 —— 原文说的是「reduces energy consumption（减少能耗）」，不是「decreased energy production（减少能源生产）」，偷换概念。

14. What do dry farmers need to do to overcome the challenges?

  • A. To create driver engagement.
  • B. To change weather conditions.
  • C. To advocate soil conservation.
  • D. To update dry farming practices.

答案与解析

答案：D

定位句：*They suggest the need for dry farmers to constantly update their knowledge of agriculture and learning of farming techniques*。

逐字稿点出 C 项（soil conservation）只是原文「read the signs of soil」的变形，不是「克服挑战」的做法。

15. The passage is mostly written by ______.

  • A. a gardener
  • B. a researcher
  • C. a historian
  • D. a salesperson

答案与解析

答案：B（researcher）

文章结构是「定义 → 历史渊源 → 效益（ Firstly / Additionally / Finally ）→ 挑战 → 结论」，

通篇引用客观事实与数据（如「more than 8,000 years」），是典型的说明性研究文体。

C（historian）是最大干扰项 —— 历史只是文中一小段佐证，不是全文主旨。

正文：

  • A. Of all the movies we will choose the old one.
  • B. But going back to old movies frees us from such trouble.
  • C. Finally, old movies allow us to relax with minimum effort.

答案与解析（16–20 全部确认）

16 → A　17 → B　18 → E　19 → D　20 → C

★ 一条送分规律（UP 主原话）：

「五选五是一对一的，一个萝卜一个坑，没有其他的迷惑项。然后你会发现 E 选项也是大写，ABCDE 其余都是大写，只有 D 是小写。
那我们来看一看谁能挂一个小写呢：16 句号在前面，17 句号在前面，18 句号在前面，19 前面是个逗号，20 前面啥都没有，只能选 19。
也就是说这也是送分题，19 题直接把 D 挂。」

逐题判定：

  • 16 → A：空后出现 *this old movie*、*it* 等代词，前面必须先提到老电影；只有 A 项（在所有电影中我们会选老片）满足。
  • 17 → B：前文讲「决策麻烦」（decision-making / spend hours），B 项 *frees us from such trouble* 中的 such trouble 正好回指该麻烦。
  • 18 → E：前文 *We know exactly what is going to happen*，E 项「即使情节令人震惊、结尾悲伤，我们也不会被打扰」承接下来，并接住后句 *This can give us a sense of certainty*。
  • 19 → D：空后为 *Old movies' content can bring back memories…*，D 项「那是因为老电影让我们想起初次观看时的自己」正是对「带回回忆」的解释；且语法上只有 D 以小写开头，而 19 空前是逗号（Besides, 19 ______）。
  • 20 → C：其余选项已用完，且 *Finally* 与前文 First / Second / Besides 构成最后一条的序列；空后「看新片费力、看老片无压力」正是「以最小努力放松」的展开。

逐题要点（UP 主讲解摘要）

  • 36：出现动词先考虑时态语态 + 主谓一致；前有「Every time」时间状语，且全段为现在时三单 → opens。
  • 37：get + 过去分词表示被动/状态，get lost（不会丢失）。
  • 38：后句「她随时可以回头再看这些未完成的作品」是前句「不着急」的原因 → because。
  • 39：with the help of 是固定搭配，属送分题。
  • 40：介词后须接名词性成分，she 的名词性形式是 her。
  • 41：句中已有谓语 uses，第二动词须用非谓语；「使用软件」与「加图」是目的关系 → 不定式 To add（表目的）。
  • 42：more than a social activity —— 「不止是一个社交活动」。
  • 43：some of her poems，不止一首 → 加 s。
  • 44：修饰 be 动词（或整句）须用副词 → usually。
  • 45：when 引导的状语中省略主语 + be，用现在分词表主动进行 → performing。
